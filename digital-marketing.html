Rising Stars Muay Thai — Brand Launch & Campaign Analytics

I designed, built, and deployed the full website for Rising Stars Muay Thai and then ran an end-to-end digital analytics engagement across two live event cycles.

Web Development & Measurement Implementation

  • Built a responsive, audience-focused site from scratch and deployed it to production.
  • Configured Google Tag Manager with a complete event taxonomy: page views, scroll depth, form submissions, button clicks, and custom conversion events (ticket purchases, PPV signups).
  • Wired GA4 to capture the full on-site customer journey with custom dimensions for event type, traffic medium, and device category.
  • Established KPIs and reporting cadence aligned to ticket and PPV revenue goals.

Campaign Analysis & Growth Results

Within two months of launch, the site grew to 3,500+ unique visitors through a combination of organic search, social referral, and direct traffic from fight promotion.

Ongoing analytics work covered:

  • Channel quality analysis — volume vs. engagement rate by acquisition source (organic, paid, social, direct)
  • Device-level conversion rates — identifying mobile vs. desktop conversion gaps
  • Pre- vs. post-event traffic patterns — quantifying the “announcement spike” and measuring decay curves across event cycles
  • Lead generation funnel — form submission rates for fighter applications and newsletter sign-ups

SEO Strategy

  • Conducted keyword research aligned to local search intent (gyms, martial arts, events in the target market).
  • Structured page metadata, heading hierarchy, and internal linking to improve crawlability and topical authority.
  • Monitored organic impressions and click-through rates in Google Search Console to refine on-page optimizations.

SEO Competitive Analysis — Trauma Resource Institute

I conducted a comprehensive SEO audit for the Trauma Resource Institute (TRI) and benchmarked their performance against key industry competitors.

Approach

  • Web scraping — collected on-page SEO signals (title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, page speed proxies, and content length) across TRI and competitor domains.
  • Keyword research — mapped the competitive keyword landscape for trauma-informed therapy and practitioner training.
  • Gap analysis — identified content gaps and structural weaknesses relative to competitors ranking for high-intent queries.

Deliverable

An interactive HTML report built in R with Quarto, featuring:

  • Side-by-side competitor comparison tables
  • Keyword opportunity visualizations
  • Prioritized, actionable recommendations for improving TRI’s organic search presence

Enterprise Content Management — Cascade CMS Automation

As part of a web operations engagement, I built a Python-based automation layer on top of the Cascade CMS REST API to transform manual content auditing and bulk publishing into scripted, repeatable processes.

What Was Built

  • Intelligent API Crawler (discovery.py) — a queue-based crawler that traverses complex folder hierarchies, extracts comprehensive asset metadata, and writes structured CSV inventory reports. Rate-limiting prevents server overload during large-scale crawls.
  • Headless CLI for AI & Automation (ai_client.py) — a machine-to-machine client designed for automated pipelines and AI-agent consumption. Outputs clean JSON to stdout (parseable by downstream systems) while directing all progress logs to stderr. Handles recursive traversal of deeply nested structuredDataNodes, programmatic workflow resolution, and batch metadata edits across entire sites.

Digital Marketing Impact

  • Turned multi-hour manual content audits into seconds of script execution.
  • Enabled AI models and automated scripts to read, modify, and publish CMS content without human intervention — a foundation for scalable content operations.
  • Delivered a reusable internal tool that reduced operational overhead for ongoing digital content management.
