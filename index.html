About

I’m completing my M.S. in Digital Marketing at Cal Poly Pomona (May 2026), where I was named CBA Student of the Year 2026. Before graduate school, I spent three years as an Associate Analyst at Robert D. Niehaus, Inc., building and maintaining R-based data pipelines for housing market analysis across 300+ U.S. military installations.

My technical foundation spans R, Python, SQL, and the full modern marketing analytics stack — GTM, GA4, BigQuery, Shiny, and Quarto. I’ve used these tools to build client-facing pipelines, geospatial market analysis applications, and reproducible research curricula.

I founded OptiMark, a university-recognized student organization at Cal Poly Pomona dedicated to digital marketing education and professional development, and I’m actively seeking opportunities to teach, consult, and collaborate at the intersection of data and marketing.
